# Data Power Source About page design

## Design read

A trust-first About page for facility leaders, general contractors, and commercial buyers. The page uses the established DPS navy and cobalt system, documentary photography, measured asymmetry, and restrained motion.

- Design variance: 6
- Motion intensity: 4
- Visual density: 5
- Theme: light-first with deliberate navy authority bands
- Component foundation: the existing Tailwind and Radix system

## Safety presentation

The safety wall distinguishes verified facts from formal certifications. EMR and NFPA 70E receive the strongest hierarchy. The remaining topics are labeled as program coverage, training, field practice, qualified operation, or workplace policy based on the source page.

The program controls are:

- Written safety program
- Regular safety meetings
- Spot safety checks
- PPE issued to new hires

The ten source-backed topics are:

- NFPA 70E arc-flash procedures
- Bloodborne pathogens
- CPR
- Crane and lifting safety
- First aid
- OSHA 10
- Aerial-lift operation
- Safe digging
- Trench and excavation safety
- Drug- and alcohol-free workplace

## Portrait treatment

The original 159 by 117 pixel Robert Kent image is retained as the identity reference. The enhanced project asset preserves age, expression, facial proportions, hairline, and plaid shirt while creating a higher-resolution head-and-shoulders portrait suitable for the page.
